# Hero copy — options to choose from

Pick one, or mix a headline from one with a lede from another. Once you choose
I'll wire it into `messages/en.json` + `messages/ar.json` and write the Arabic
to match the register you pick.

---

## What's wrong with the current copy

```
kingdom of flavour

718 restaurants, cafés and suppliers — one association speaking for
the sector that feeds a country and welcomes the world.
```

Three specific tells, not a vague vibe:

1. **The em-dash pivot into a grand abstraction.** "…— one association speaking
   for the sector that feeds a country and welcomes the world." The sentence
   starts concrete (a number) and escapes into something unfalsifiable.
2. **The rule of three, unearned.** "restaurants, cafés and suppliers" then
   "feeds a country and welcomes the world" — two triads in one sentence.
3. **It never says what the site does.** A first-time visitor cannot tell from
   the hero whether this is a tourism campaign, a magazine, or a directory.

"kingdom of flavour" itself is defensible — it's the name of the whole design
direction and it's genuinely evocative. The lede is where the damage is.

---

## Option 1 — Institutional

```
Jordan's restaurants, in one place

The official directory of 718 member restaurants and 39 suppliers,
with the licensing, classification and training the sector runs on.
```

Reads like the sector's governing body. Lists the four things the site actually
holds. **Best if** the primary audience is members and government.
*Trade-off:* loses all warmth; could be any trade association.

---

## Option 2 — Keep the line, fix the lede

```
kingdom of flavour

718 member restaurants and 39 suppliers across 17 governorates.
Search the directory, or apply for membership.
```

Keeps Direction B's signature and follows it with facts and two clear actions.
**Best if** you want to keep the brand line you already chose.
*Trade-off:* the headline still does no informational work.

---

## Option 3 — Task-first

```
Find a restaurant. Find a supplier.

The official directory of the Jordan Restaurant Association.
718 members, 39 suppliers, 17 governorates.
```

Names the two jobs people come to do, in the order they come to do them.
Strongest match for a directory product, and it pairs naturally with the search
console sitting directly beneath.
*Trade-off:* the least distinctive of the five; reads like a utility.

---

## Option 4 — Authority

```
The official body of Jordan's restaurant sector

Since 1976: the member directory, the classification standards,
and the legislation that governs 718 establishments.
```

Leads with mandate rather than volume. The date does real work — an association
founded in 1976 is not a startup directory.
**Best if** the site's job is to establish that JRA is *the* authority.
*Trade-off:* "Since 1976" in the lede is a slightly conventional move.

---

## Option 5 — Plain and confident

```
Every restaurant in Jordan worth knowing about

718 members, 39 suppliers, 17 governorates. Run by the association
that represents them.
```

A claim with a point of view, then the numbers that back it, then the
credential. Shortest of the five.
**Best if** you want personality without the tourism-brochure register.
*Trade-off:* "worth knowing about" is a value judgement the association may not
want to make about non-members — worth checking, since every non-member
restaurant in Jordan is a potential member.

---

## My recommendation

**Option 3 for the headline, Option 4's second sentence for the lede:**

```
Find a restaurant. Find a supplier.

The official body of Jordan's restaurant sector since 1976.
718 members, 39 suppliers, 17 governorates.
```

Tasks first because that is what the page is for, then the credential that says
why this directory rather than Google Maps. No em-dash, no triad, nothing that
cannot be checked.
